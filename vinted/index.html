Rules and postage checks

Categories are suggestions, not guaranteed acceptance. Vinted's UK rules include electronics, small kitchen appliances, books and craft supplies, but exclude some products, including security cameras and electronics not intended for the EEA market. A video doorbell needs its own category check. Imported Japanese consoles and a known broken-screen laptop are not treated as ready to list.

Use protective packaging appropriate to the actual item. A mailing bag alone does not protect electronics or sharp tools. Check the sealed parcel's packed dimensions and weight against the service Vinted offers before choosing postage. No parcel size or carrier fit has been assumed.
